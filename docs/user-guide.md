# Learner guide

The interface and bundled lessons are in English. Your profile's native language controls AI vocabulary translations.

## First visit

Start the deployment using the [README](../README.md#quick-start-with-docker). The installation operator completes /setup and chooses shared AI/voice services. Create a separate learner account afterward. The installation password does not log in to learner accounts.

Set your native language, target CEFR level and daily time goal in Profile. Core reading, grammar, vocabulary and reviews work without AI. Speaking, generated lessons and new AI explanations require configured services.

## Read and translate

Open a text in Texts. Click a word or select a short phrase to see its context, glossary/AI explanation, translation and speaker controls. Save useful words to your vocabulary.

Word cards also work on rendered text across other pages. In a writing field, select text and use Translate selected text or Alt+T. Ordinary clicks in form controls do not trigger translation. Password and credential fields are excluded. Lookup is limited to six words / 80 characters; long selections must be shortened.

A word selected outside an original reading text is saved without inventing a source-text link. Without a working AI provider, only an available source glossary can supply an explanation.

## Build your vocabulary

Use Add word for your own term, translation, definition and example. Edit existing entries and assign categories such as food or clothing. Search your collection and optionally attach a picture.

Card display settings control which information is shown first and after revealing the answer. Select translation-first practice if you want to recall the English term from a Ukrainian or other native-language meaning. Settings change the presentation, not the stored word.

In Review, try recalling the answer before revealing it. Rate Again, Hard, Good or Easy honestly; FSRS schedules the next review. Vocabulary practice provides a separate gap-fill activity.

## Practise skills

- **Grammar:** filter or search topics, read the patterns and complete checked exercises. Attempts and topic progress are saved.
- **Speaking:** start a teacher call with a working AI provider, local recognition and microphone permission. Replies are processed in turns; you can pause or interrupt playback. Use localhost or HTTPS.
- **Writing:** exchange emails with the AI pen pal. Corrections link to explanations; higher levels can show more detail. The exchange remains in the current page session.
- **Listening:** generate narrated lessons with questions, then review evidence/transcripts. The video section supports publisher links and local video/subtitle files; local files stay in the browser.
- **Exams:** complete short IELTS-style practice. Results are practice estimates rather than official IELTS or certified CEFR scores. Temporary listening/exam sessions expire after 24 hours.

## Follow your progress

Profile shows a daily plan matched to your time goal, six skill XP tracks and recent activity. Continue learning checks what is unfinished and opens the next available activity. Timers preserve remaining time and synchronize recorded totals; elapsed time alone earns no XP.

The weekly report uses Monday-Sunday in Europe/Kyiv and allows earlier-week comparisons. XP/player levels describe practice progress, not measured language proficiency.

Mistakes collects captured grammar, writing and speaking corrections. Filter entries, study the rule and mark an item understood, or reopen it. This journal is separate from FSRS word reviews.

## Voice speed and privacy

Repeated speech can reuse an already loaded clip. Selected words may prepare audio before you press play, depending on the configured voice and your sharing settings. The first new recording can still take seconds; local models also need initial downloads/warm-up.

External AI requires both the operator's permission and your profile consent. External sentence speech requires consent; explicit single-term pronunciation has a separate policy. Automatic external speech preparation requires consent and does not speculatively call paid Azure. Browser online voices can also use an external service; private sentence fallback is restricted to installed local voices.

Export your account data or delete your account from Profile. Deletion requires your learner password. Learner forgotten-password recovery is not implemented yet; the operator's installation-password reset is a different procedure.
