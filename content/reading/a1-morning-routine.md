My name is Tom. I live in a small flat in the city. I work in a bookshop.

I wake up at seven o'clock every day. First, I open the window. Then I go to the bathroom. I wash my face and brush my teeth.

I usually have breakfast at half past seven. I eat bread with cheese and I drink a cup of tea. My cat sits on the chair next to me. She wants some cheese too!

After breakfast, I get dressed. I put on a shirt, trousers and black shoes. I take my bag and my keys.

I leave home at eight o'clock. I walk to the bus stop. The bus comes at ten past eight. I am never late for work.
