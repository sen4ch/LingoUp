## Meaning and use

English uses several constructions traditionally grouped under the subjunctive label. Mandative plain clauses express proposed or required situations; irrealis were marks a remote alternative in certain singular contexts; fixed expressions preserve other conventional patterns. These constructions are not all one productive verb tense. Formality, dialect, and meaning affect the available alternatives, so distinguish the grammatical pattern from the teaching label.

## Forms and sentence patterns

- Mandative plain form: They demanded that he be heard.
- Remote alternative: If she were available, we could proceed.
- Formulaic pattern: Long live the association.
- Formal alternatives include should clauses; ordinary indicative clauses can convey a different reading.

## Examples in context

> It is imperative that no applicant be disadvantaged.
> I wish the decision were easier to explain.
> Be that as it may, the original agreement remains valid.

## Common mistakes and important distinctions

Were is not a mandatory replacement for every past was: If she was there yesterday can investigate a real past possibility. A mandative clause uses the plain verb even after a past reporting verb. Do not automatically change factual reporting into a recommendation: The evidence suggests that he is abroad is not a demand that he be abroad.
