## Meaning and use

Discourse markers guide a reader through additions, contrasts, conclusions, examples, and changes of topic. They express relationships between ideas rather than merely decorating sentences. Linking adverbs such as nevertheless and consequently are not ordinary coordinating conjunctions. Spoken markers such as well and anyway also manage turns and transitions. Choose a marker only after deciding which relationship the argument actually contains.

## Forms and sentence patterns

- Addition: furthermore, moreover, in addition.
- Contrast/concession: however, nevertheless, even so.
- Result/inference: consequently, therefore, thus.
- Example/reformulation: for example, in other words, that is.

## Examples in context

> The sample was small. Nevertheless, the pattern was consistent.
> Two records were missing; consequently, the total was revised.
> The system stores data locally—in other words, on this device.

## Common mistakes and important distinctions

A comma alone normally cannot join two independent clauses with however or therefore. Too many markers can make a short paragraph feel mechanical. For example illustrates a claim; in other words restates one. On the contrary rejects a previous proposition, while on the other hand typically presents another aspect. Check logic as well as punctuation.
