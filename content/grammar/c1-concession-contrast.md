## Meaning and use

Concessive clauses acknowledge a fact that might suggest a different result. Although and even though explicitly concede a proposition; even if concedes a possibility rather than necessarily a fact. While and whereas can contrast two situations. However plus an adjective or adverb can mean no matter how. More formal patterns include adjective + though/as + clause, and much as expressing a concessive attitude.

## Forms and sentence patterns

- Even though + fact; even if + possible condition.
- However + adjective/adverb + subject + verb.
- Tired though she was, she continued.
- While/whereas introduces a contrast between clauses.

## Examples in context

> However carefully we checked, small errors remained.
> Much as I like the design, I cannot afford it.
> Tired though he was, he stayed until the end.

## Common mistakes and important distinctions

Distinguish linking-adverb however from concessive however: However, we continued has a different structure from However tired we were, we continued. No matter how needs a complete degree clause rather than a bare adjective alone. Do not normally duplicate although with but. Use a more familiar structure when a formal inversion would distract from the message.
