## Meaning and use

A reflexive pronoun often refers back to the subject within the same clause: the person doing the action is also affected by it. An emphatic reflexive highlights who performed an action. By myself can mean alone or without help. Reciprocal expressions such as each other describe mutual actions between participants rather than an action directed back at the same individual.

## Forms and sentence patterns

- Reflexives: myself, yourself, himself, herself, itself, ourselves, yourselves, themselves.
- Emphasis: The director herself answered.
- Alone: We built it by ourselves.
- Reciprocal: They helped each other.

## Examples in context

> I cut myself while chopping vegetables.
> The children introduced themselves.
> Maya and Leo send each other postcards.

## Common mistakes and important distinctions

Do not replace ordinary I or me with myself just to sound formal: Please contact me is usually right. Reflexives do not normally belong after every personal-care verb: I washed and dressed is natural. They blamed themselves and They blamed each other have different meanings. Singular they can be followed by themselves; themself is also used in some contexts.
