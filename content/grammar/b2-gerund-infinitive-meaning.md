## Meaning and use

Some verbs allow either an -ing form or a to infinitive with a change in meaning. Remember doing looks back at an action held in memory; remember to do concerns remembering a task before performing it. Stop doing ends an activity, while stop to do interrupts one activity for a purpose. Try doing tests a possible method; try to do describes an effort to achieve a goal. The two patterns are related, not arbitrary synonyms.

## Forms and sentence patterns

- Remember/forget doing: memory of an earlier event.
- Remember/forget to do: remembering or failing to perform a required action.
- Stop doing versus stop to do something else.
- Regret doing a past action; regret to inform for unwelcome information.

## Examples in context

> I remember locking the door.
> Please remember to lock the door when you leave.
> Try restarting the app if the screen freezes.

## Common mistakes and important distinctions

Do not treat the -ing form as always present and the infinitive as always future. The meaning comes from the complete verb pattern. Go on doing continues an activity; go on to do moves to a new one. Mean doing describes an implication, while mean to do expresses intention. Context can make more than one pattern grammatically possible with different meanings.
