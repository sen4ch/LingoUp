## Meaning and use

Article choice depends on identifiability, countability, and whether the noun phrase refers generally or specifically. The can identify something unique in a situation, shared knowledge, or an item defined by a following phrase. A/an classifies one singular countable item. Zero article is common with general plurals and uncountable nouns, many proper names, and some institutional expressions. Proper-name patterns have exceptions that should be learned with the name.

## Forms and sentence patterns

- General: Music matters. Specific: The music in that scene was beautiful.
- Usually no article: France, Mount Fuji, Lake Victoria.
- Common the patterns: rivers, seas, mountain ranges, and plural country names.
- Institutional activity: at school, in prison; a particular building may take the.

## Examples in context

> The moon was bright above the city.
> They crossed the Atlantic in winter.
> My daughter goes to school near the hospital.

## Common mistakes and important distinctions

A following of phrase does not automatically require the; a member of a club is still indefinite. Do not mechanically add the before every country or language. Meals and languages often have zero article in general references: have breakfast, speak English. Institutional expressions vary by dialect and meaning, especially in hospital versus in the hospital.
